# Source and provider intake

Reviewed candidate documentation on November 23, 2026. This does not grant rights or approval to ingest documents. USGS is an opt-in supplementary adapter with live connectivity verified; PAGASA and PHIVOLCS are linked, not ingested. Brave is optional; authenticated service access was not verified. See the [external integration guide](guides/05-external-api-integrations.md). The [source workflow](guides/02-document-ingestion.md) was verified with synthetic fixtures in isolated stores only.

## Review before indexing

Record title, publisher, official URL, version, publication and retrieval dates, jurisdiction, hazard, permissions and approver. Verify supersession, extracted page anchors and advice with sample questions. Publish only approved versions. In conflicts, match authority to the question; do not choose a local source over a national weather warning merely because it is local. Broad susceptibility never establishes safety for a house or road.

External providers start off. Use a fixed-host, bounded, timed client and a separate allowlisted document fetch policy; fail closed on invalid/redirected URLs or stale/unverifiable data. Show observation and verification times distinctly. Do not infer that a warning is absent when its provider fails.
